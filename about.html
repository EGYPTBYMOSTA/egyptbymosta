My Story

Welcome to Egypt By Mosta!

I'm Mostafa Hassan, an Egyptian tourism professional, travel consultant, content creator and marketing manager with a passion for travel, storytelling and Egyptian heritage.

My journey brings together tourism, digital marketing and a deep interest in Egypt's ancient civilization, rich culture and extraordinary people.

What I Do

Through my work in tourism, I help travelers discover Egypt through authentic experiences, cultural journeys and unforgettable adventures.

Through Egypt By Mosta, I share hidden gems, historical facts, travel inspiration and stories that introduce the world to a different side of Egypt.

My Mission

My mission is to showcase the true beauty and greatness of Egypt, celebrate its civilization and people, and inspire travelers to look beyond the famous landmarks.

I believe Egypt is more than a destination. It is a living civilization, a unique identity and an endless story waiting to be discovered.

My Story

Welcome to Egypt By Mosta!

I'm Mostafa Hassan, an Egyptian tourism professional, travel consultant and content creator passionate about showcasing the beauty, history and culture of Egypt.

My journey combines tourism, digital marketing and storytelling. Through my work, I connect travelers with authentic Egyptian experiences and help them discover places beyond the usual tourist destinations.

What I Do

I work in tourism and travel experiences, helping visitors explore Egypt through thoughtfully organized tours, cultural discoveries and memorable journeys.

As a content creator, I share hidden gems, historical facts, travel tips and stories about the people and places that make Egypt special.

My Mission

My mission is simple: to show the world the real Egypt. Its civilization, its people, its incredible destinations and the stories that deserve to be told.

Egypt is more than a destination. It's a feeling, a history and an experience waiting to be discovered.
